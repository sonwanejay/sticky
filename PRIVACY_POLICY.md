# Privacy Policy for Sticky

**Last updated:** June 13, 2026

## Overview

Sticky is a note-taking app that stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## Data Collection

We do **not** collect any data. Specifically:

- No personal information (name, email, phone number)
- No usage analytics or tracking
- No crash reports
- No cookies or identifiers
- No location data

## Data Storage

All notes and app preferences are stored locally on your device. No data is sent to any server or third party.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services that collect data.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
